# Design and Research first!
The best way to make an effective and beautiful website with lefolio, as with any other tool, is to first design what your website will be about and to do a bit of research.
- Think about the value you want to showcase
- Think about what actions you want visitors to take when they visit your site
- Gather material that defines you: screenshots, images, logos, etc.
- Gather links to a few sites that you like and would like to look like
