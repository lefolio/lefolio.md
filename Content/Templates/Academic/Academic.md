The Academic template is designed for researchers with a clean, readable style and project and publication listings.
## Publication list with thumbnails
Showcase your publications with *very clickable* thumbnails. Surface the beauty of your work by pulling your most beautiful visuals from your papers to the surface.
![[publication_list.png|500|center]]
## Project grid with large thumbnails
Your ongoing or past projects also have beautiful figures and diagrams to show; make them visible with the project grid with large thumbnails.
